# Content Studio — how the app works

This file is the source of truth for the in-app support chatbot. Use only these facts. Do not invent emails, phone numbers, WhatsApp, payment links, or cash-out. Coins are not money. Never ask for a password. The on-screen language is English. Titles, captions, and scripts can be English or Malayalam.

## What Content Studio is
Kind: help
Keywords: what is, content studio, this app, this site, youtube, instagram, workspace, private, about the app, what can i do

Content Studio is a private workspace for planning YouTube and Instagram videos. Each signed-in account sees only its own ideas, drafts, calendar, prompts, hashtags, shop buys, and coins. You can write titles and captions, plan a week of ideas, save publishing dates, copy thumbnail prompts into Google Flow, shop digital products, and earn in-app coins in Fun mode.

## Sign in and accounts
Kind: help
Keywords: sign in, login, log in, password, register, create an account, account, forgot, incorrect, email, reset password

Use Sign in with your email and password, or Create an account. A new password needs at least 8 characters, including one letter and one number. If you cannot get in, open Forgot password and use the email link. After 14 days, or 7 days without a visit, the app signs you out. That is normal. Sign in again to keep working. Your content stays on your account.

## Session length
Kind: help
Keywords: session, timeout, logged out, sign out, expire, timed out, many days, kicked out

A session lasts 14 days from the last sign-in. If you do not open the app for 7 days, it also signs you out. Sign in again. Nothing on the account is deleted by a timeout.

## Finding pages on phone and desktop
Kind: help
Keywords: zoom, mobile, phone, responsive, small screen, hamburger, menu, navigation, sidebar, bottom bar, where is, open calendar

On a computer, the left sidebar lists Plan, Create, Discover, Profile, Purchases, and Settings. Your display name is at the top of the studio and in the account card. On a phone, Home, Content, Shop, and Play sit on the bottom bar. Open Menu (☰) for Calendar, Writing, Planner, Library, Thumbnails, Hashtags, Affiliate ads, Profile, Purchases, and Settings. Buttons are large enough to tap. The calendar week grid scrolls sideways on purpose.

## Dashboard
Kind: help
Keywords: dashboard, home, at a glance, counts, recent, overview, workspace home

Dashboard is the home page after sign-in. It shows how many items are planned, draft, ready, scheduled, or published, plus YouTube and Instagram counts. Recent work, upcoming dates, and your coin balance are there. Use New content, or the chips for Planner, Thumbnails, Calendar, Marketplace, and Fun mode.

## Content
Kind: help
Keywords: content, new content, draft, duplicate, delete, status, idea, published, filter, search titles, video url

Open Content to search, filter, and sort your ideas. New content starts with a title. Status moves Idea → Planning → In progress → Ready → Scheduled → Published. You can set YouTube, Instagram, or both, and English or Malayalam. Duplicate copies a row. Delete asks for confirmation. Saved video links must start with https://.

## Writing assistant
Kind: help
Keywords: writing, assistant, caption, description, hook, script outline, generate writing, titles, tags

Writing (also labelled Writing assistant) drafts titles, descriptions, captions, tags, hashtags, a hook, and a script outline. Pick English or Malayalam, and YouTube, Instagram, or both. Generate needs a signed-in account on the live site. You can edit every field, copy it, or save it onto a content item. This chat cannot run Generate for you.

## Idea planner
Kind: help
Keywords: planner, plan ideas, 7 day, week of videos, how to shoot, audio script, comedy, nostalgia

Planner builds 1 to 7 days of video ideas. Each idea is a title plus how to shoot it. Themes include comedy, nostalgia, tech, education, food, lifestyle, and family. Lengths run from 15 seconds to 5 minutes. An audio script is written only when you tap that button on one idea. Edit anything, then save ideas onto the calendar.

## Calendar
Kind: help
Keywords: calendar, schedule, week, month, undated, drag, publishing date

Open Calendar from the Plan menu (or Menu on a phone). Drag a content card onto a day, or pick a date on the card. Switch month and week views. On a phone, swipe the week grid sideways. Undated items sit under the grid.

## Library
Kind: help
Keywords: library, published links, drafts, references, browse saved

Library is a browse view of what you already saved: published links, draft videos, thumbnails, descriptions, ideas, reference links, and hashtag collections. It does not replace Content, where you edit a row.

## Thumbnail prompts
Kind: help
Keywords: thumbnail, flow, google flow, prompt, picture, 16:9, 9:16, landscape, vertical, image file

Thumbnails writes English prompts you paste into Google Flow (landscape 16:9 and vertical 9:16). This app does not create the image file. Pick a saved title or type a topic. Copy the prompt, then save it on a content item if you want it in the library. You can also upload a JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF thumbnail up to 5 MB on a content item.

## Hashtags
Kind: help
Keywords: hashtag collection, reusable tags, hashtags page

Hashtags stores reusable collections you can drop onto content. Writing and Affiliate ads can also generate hashtags for one post. Copy buttons sit next to those fields.

## Affiliate ads
Kind: help
Keywords: affiliate, product link, meesho, clothing, jewellery, shoes, electronics, image prompt, video prompt, ad caption

Affiliate ads is a separate workspace for product posts. Paste a full https product link, or type the product name. You get a caption, hashtags, and image and video prompts for clothing, jewellery, shoes, electronics, beauty, home, or other. If a preview cannot load, paste the complete https URL, or generate from the name without a preview.

## Marketplace
Kind: help
Keywords: market, shop, marketplace, skill, digital product, buy, checkout, product grid, listed, sell

Marketplace is a product grid of skills and other digital files. Open a card for price, what is included, ratings, and short reviews. Pay with studio coins, or use Buy now when rupee checkout is available. Razorpay card and UPI checkout is coming soon. Free listings can be taken now. Downloads do not stay on the shop floor — they move to Purchases.

## Ratings and reviews
Kind: help
Keywords: review, rating, star, feedback, comment on product, leave a review

After you buy a product (status paid), open it and leave 1–5 stars plus a short review. Reviews need 8 to 280 characters. You can update your own review later. The name shown is your display name, or Customer — never your email. You cannot review a product you have not bought.

## Purchases and downloads
Kind: help
Keywords: purchase, download, my files, owned, where are my downloads, bought

Purchases holds files you already paid for or claimed. Download them any time from that page. The marketplace is only for browsing and buying. If a download fails, sign in again and open Purchases, not the product grid.

## Studio coins and Fun mode
Kind: help
Keywords: coin, fun mode, play, wallet, rupee, cash, withdraw, memory, catch, spark, claim

Studio coins are an in-app reward, not money. They cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for cash. 100 coins = ₹1 only when you redeem them in this marketplace. Play Fun mode (Memory marathon, Catch the reel, Spark rush) for at least 20 seconds, then Claim coins. A run can add up to 12 coins. The daily cap is 400 coins. Your earned total and spendable balance show on Dashboard, Fun mode, and Settings.

## Coin packs
Kind: help
Keywords: coin pack, buy coins, razorpay, starter, studio pack, pro pack, bundle, pay with money

On Fun mode you can buy coin packs with real money: Starter 2,000 coins, Studio 5,000, Pro 10,000, Bundle 25,000, at 100 coins per ₹1. Razorpay checkout is coming soon, so Pay may show that it cannot start yet. Packs credit only this in-app balance. You can still earn coins by playing.

## Copy titles, tags, and descriptions
Kind: help
Keywords: copy, clipboard, copy button, copy the description, copy tags, copy title, copy caption

Title, description, caption, tag, and hashtag fields have a copy button on the right of the label. Writing, Planner, Thumbnails, Library, and Affiliate ads also copy those texts. Tap or press Enter on the copy button. If the button is blocked, select the text and copy it with your phone or browser menu.

## Languages
Kind: help
Keywords: malayalam, english, language, bilingual, ml

The buttons and menus stay in English. Content fields can be Malayalam or English. Writing, Planner, and Affiliate ads let you pick the language before Generate. Thumbnail prompts stay in English so Google Flow can follow them. A Malayalam topic is quoted as the words to place on the thumbnail.

## Generate
Kind: help
Keywords: generate, ai, gemini, openai, draft for me, write for me, busy, quota

Generate writes thumbnail prompts, writing drafts, topic copy, plans, audio scripts, and affiliate ads. On the live site you must be signed in. Enter a topic or title first. You can edit every result before you save. Studio coins do not pay for Generate. If the model is busy, wait a minute and tap Generate again. You can always type the fields by hand.

## Profile
Kind: help
Keywords: profile, my email, my name, username, display name, who am i, account details

Profile shows your display name at the top, plus your email, studio coins, whether you have the seller desk, and when the account was created. Open Profile from the Account menu or tap your name at the top. You can change the display name there. Other people cannot see this page.

## Settings
Kind: help
Keywords: settings, display name, profile, category, csv, backup, restore, export

Settings holds content categories, coin balance, CSV export, and a JSON backup. Your name and email are on Profile. CSV and JSON include only your account. Thumbnail image files are not inside the JSON backup. Restore adds rows; it does not delete what is already there. Categories organise content; deleting a category leaves the content.

## Light and dark
Kind: help
Keywords: dark mode, light mode, theme, appearance, sun, moon, black, red, netflix

Use the sun or moon button in the header to switch light and dark. Both themes use black, charcoal, and red, with Inter-style type. The choice is saved on this browser.

## Privacy
Kind: help
Keywords: private, other people, who can see, rls, my data, security, someone else

Your ideas, files, calendar, and wallet belong to your signed-in account. Other customers cannot open them. Saved links must start with https://. This chat never needs your password. Do not paste passwords, OTPs, or bank details here.

## Keyboard and contrast
Kind: help
Keywords: keyboard, tab, screen reader, contrast, font, accessible, skip

You can move through the studio with Tab and Enter. Skip to content jumps past the header. Text uses sans-serif fonts at 16px or larger, with high contrast on light and dark backgrounds. Press Escape to close the menu or this chat.

## This support chat
Kind: help
Keywords: chatbot, this chat, support chat, headset, help icon, 24 hours, 24/7

The headset button opens this chat. You can drag it. It answers around the clock from this working guide. Keep a message under 500 characters. Ask for the support email or phone if you want a person — those details are an example desk until a live inbox is set.

## General errors
Kind: error
Keywords: error, bug, broken, failed, fail, crash, not working, doesn't work, does not work, won't work, something went wrong, could not, cannot, can't, issue, problem, stuck, blank, loading, try again, went wrong

If you see “Something went wrong”, tap Try again or refresh the page. Note the page name and the exact red message. Most studio errors are sign-in, Generate, save, upload, copy, shop, or a slow connection. Say which of those it is and I will give the next step. Do not send your password in this chat.

## Sign-in errors
Kind: error
Keywords: incorrect, confirm your email, too many attempts, reset, forgot password, sign in to continue, timed out, session timed, invalid login

“Email or password is incorrect” means the password does not match that email. Use Forgot password, then open the email link. If you just registered, confirm the email first. “Too many attempts” means wait a few minutes. “Your session timed out” or “Sign in to continue” means sign in again. Content on the account is not deleted.

## Generate and AI errors
Kind: error
Keywords: generate, gemini, ai provider, api settings, quota, busy, resource_exhausted, not available, unknown generator, incomplete

Generate needs a signed-in account on the live site. If it says the AI provider could not write, wait a minute and tap Generate again — the model is often busy. Check you entered a topic or title first. You can still type titles, captions, and tags by hand and save. This chat cannot run Generate for you.

## Save, load, and upload errors
Kind: error
Keywords: could not save, could not load, could not upload, could not create, could not delete, could not move, could not find, thumbnail, upload

“Could not save” usually means the session dropped. Sign in again, then save once more. If a page will not load, refresh and check your connection. Thumbnail upload needs a normal image file; if it fails, try a smaller JPG or PNG, then save the content. Duplicate and templates need the same signed-in account.

## Shop, coins, and download errors
Kind: error
Keywords: could not buy, could not claim, could not start checkout, not on sale, not enough, wallet, razorpay, purchase, download

Coin redeem fails if the wallet is short or the product is not on sale. Play Fun mode for at least 20 seconds before claiming coins. Downloads live in Purchases, not on the Marketplace floor. Rupee checkout and Razorpay are coming soon, so Buy now may show that it cannot start yet. Files you already bought stay in Purchases.

## Copy and link errors
Kind: error
Keywords: could not copy, clipboard, product link, could not load that product

If copy fails, select the text and copy it with your phone or browser menu — some browsers block the copy button. Affiliate “could not load that product link” means the URL is blocked or incomplete. Paste the full https link, or type the product name and generate without a preview.
